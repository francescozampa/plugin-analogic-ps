# Product specification

## One sentence

**Analogic turns a clean digital photograph into something that feels shot, developed and scanned — without making the user think like a color scientist.**

## Product principles

### Character over simulation theater
We do not need to reproduce every chemical variable. We need the final image to feel coherent.

### Few controls, meaningful controls
Core controls: Look, Intensity, Grain, Grain Size, Halation and Bloom.

### Presets are scenarios, not SKU emulations
Avoid making the product a catalog of trademarked film names. A preset can imply stock character, exposure behavior, development, print/scan, era and camera context.

### Real source material where it matters
Use genuine scanned grain because real material contains useful irregularity. Do not assume a raw scan should be composited unchanged.

### Non-destructive by default
The user should trust the plugin inside a client PSD. Generated changes should be grouped, named and removable.

## Primary user

A designer, photographer or art director using Photoshop who wants analog character quickly but still wants control.

## MVP experience

1. Open a photo.
2. Open Analogic.
3. Choose a look.
4. Change intensity / grain / halation.
5. Click Apply.
6. Plugin creates one named group.
7. Continue editing in Photoshop.

## Launch preset families

Clean negative, Consumer color, Flash night, Pushed and Slide-ish. These are creative categories, not claims of scientific stock reproduction.

## What Analogic should not become

A giant preset browser, fake camera UI, one-click vintage gimmick, layer bomb, texture pack disguised as software or technically pure simulation that is too slow to use.
